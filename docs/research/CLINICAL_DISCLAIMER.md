## 1. Research Prototype Notice

**GlucoShield is an exploratory academic research and software prototyping platform.**

It is designed for scientific inquiry, machine learning evaluation, and clinical decision-support demonstrations only.

- **NOT AN FDA-APPROVED MEDICAL DEVICE:** GlucoShield has not been evaluated, cleared, or approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), European Medicines Agency (EMA), or any other national or regional healthcare regulatory authority.
- **NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR MEDICAL JUDGMENT:** GlucoShield predictions, confidence intervals, What-If counterfactual trajectories, and clinical alerts do **NOT** constitute medical prescriptions, diagnostic decisions, or automated insulin dosing calculations.
- **NO AUTOMATED DOSING OR CLOSED-LOOP CONTROL:** GlucoShield does not connect to automated insulin delivery (AID) systems, smart insulin pens, or automated pumps. Users must **never** adjust basal infusion rates or bolus doses based solely on predictions generated by this software.
- **CLINICAL CONSULTATION MANDATORY:** Individuals managing diabetes mellitus must always consult a certified endocrinologist, diabetologist, or credentialed healthcare professional regarding their medical regimen and glycemic management.

---

## 2. In Silico & Synthetic Demonstrations

Portions of GlucoShield's interactive decision flows (such as What-If scenario simulations, virtual meal perturbation tests, and demo patient profiles) operate on:
1. De-identified historical clinical datasets (`Shanghai Continuous Glucose Monitoring Cohort`).
2. Mathematical approximations derived from the Hovorka 8-compartment nonlinear metabolic differential equation model.

Such simulations provide educational and comparative intelligence only and may deviate from an individual patient's real-world biology under acute illness, vigorous physical exertion, psychological stress, or concomitant pharmacotherapy.
